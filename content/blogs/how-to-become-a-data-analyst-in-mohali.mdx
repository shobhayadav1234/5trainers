# How to Become a Data Analyst in Mohali

Data analytics has rapidly emerged as one of the most promising and in-demand career paths in Mohali. With the city's growing IT ecosystem, startups, multinational corporations, and digital transformation initiatives, organizations increasingly depend on skilled data analysts to make data-driven business decisions.

Mohali offers excellent educational resources, training institutes, and placement opportunities for aspiring professionals looking to enter the field of analytics.

## Why Data Analytics is a High-Growth Career

Data analysts play a critical role in:
- Business intelligence
- Sales forecasting
- Customer behavior analysis
- Operational improvements
- Strategic planning
- Financial reporting

### Major Career Benefits:
- High job demand
- Competitive salary packages
- Diverse industry applications

- Strong future growth into AI and Data Science

## Step-by-Step Roadmap

### Step 1: Master Data Fundamentals
Start by learning:
- Excel formulas
- Data formatting
- Pivot tables
- Reports
- Charts

### Step 2: Learn SQL
Important topics:
- Queries
- Joins
- Aggregations
- Data extraction
- Database optimization

### Step 3: Visualization Tools
Focus on:
- Power BI dashboards
- Tableau reports
- Business KPIs
- Reporting automation

### Step 4: Python for Data Analytics
Important libraries:
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
